# Mercy Ledger

A fan-made, local companion for tracking RAID: Shadow Legends shard and Soulstone summons.

## Get started

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. No installation, account, or game connection is required. Data is saved in that browser's local storage on this device. Browser private mode, clearing site data, changing browsers, or device loss can remove it; use **Export backup** regularly and **Import** to restore it. Import replaces the current ledger after confirmation.

## Using the tracker

- Enter your **current** number of pulls since each specific rarity directly on its summon card. Leave a field blank if the current count is unknown; zero means you know the last pull was that rarity. Update the relevant counters manually after summoning; a matching rarity resets its own counter to zero. Summon and rarity counters are independent.
- Mercy alerts appear when the next pull receives an increased chance. Epic highlights its row; Legendary highlights the card in gold; Mythical uses a stronger red-and-white card highlight. If multiple rarities are active, each retains its status text and Epic row highlight, while the card shows the highest active rarity. An active alert does not mean the pull is guaranteed. If an unknown historical count has a recorded minimum that already meets the threshold, the alert appears even though the exact chance remains unknown.
- Remnant Mythical uses the user-supplied rates: 82.5% Epic, 15% Void Epic, 2.5% Mythical; after 24 summons without a Mythical, the next summon gains 1 percentage point and each additional miss adds another point. This rule has not been independently verified; check the in-game information. Only its Mythical mercy is tracked.
- **Soulstone coin calculator:** enter your current Mortal and Immortal Soulstones and Soul Coins. The projection assumes you buy every whole Mortal stone available with Mortal coins, pull all Mortal stones for 20 Immortal coins each, and buy every whole Immortal stone with the resulting coins. Each Mortal or Immortal stone costs 1,000 coins of its own type. It displays purchasable stones, remaining coins, and total stones. Inputs default to zero and are saved locally and in backups. Previously entered Eternal values remain in saved data but are no longer displayed or used in the projection.
- This is a hypothetical calculation; no stones are pulled or purchased. It excludes silver costs, selling souls, other rewards, and any event-specific changes. For example, 204 Mortal stones and 825 Immortal coins (with all other fields zero) produce 4,080 Immortal coins, a total of 4,905, or 4 purchasable Immortal stones and 905 coins left.
- Existing history and starting counts are preserved in local storage and backups, and older version 1 through 9 backups still import. Unless you enter a current count, the app continues to calculate from that history. A directly entered count takes precedence over that historical calculation, including when cleared to unknown; it does not delete the underlying entries. The simplified dashboard does not display or edit past entries. Previous Soulstone journal totals represent pulls already recorded, not stones owned, and therefore are preserved but not copied into the new inventory.
- Each displayed next-pull chance uses standard rates and the recorded counter. Mercy increments begin on the pull after the listed threshold. The app does not calculate event-adjusted rarity odds or account for undisclosed interactions between simultaneous rarity chances. Game events, special summons, and rule changes can make the displayed estimate inaccurate. Consult the in-game odds and event details.
- **Battle points calculators:** Event and Tournament plans are independent. Enter whole numbers for points per battle, battles, energy per battle, and time per battle in minutes and seconds (seconds 0–59). Each total is the per-battle value multiplied by the number of battles; the time is shown as hours, minutes, and seconds. Inputs default to zero and are saved locally and in backups. Estimates assume consistent battle rewards, costs, and times and do not include loading or other delays.
- Select the Event, Tournament, Shards, or Souls tab to switch calculators. Inputs and results persist when switching tabs, and saved inputs remain available after reopening the app.
- **Summon points calculators:** Enter the number of summons and points per summon for each type; each row displays its product and the calculator sums its rows. Shard summons include Mystery, Ancient, Void, Sacred, Primal, Prism, and Remnant summons; Soul summons include Mortal, Immortal, Eternal, and Prism Soulstones. Remnant is a summon type, not a shard. For Prism, enter the number of summons, not the number of Prism shards consumed. Points vary by event, so enter the event's values manually. Plans save separately from mercy counters and Soulstone inventory and do not change either.
- **Demon Lord reward tracker:** Select a local calendar date and record the combined rewards from claimed chests separately for Ultra-Nightmare, Nightmare, Brutal, and Hard, including Legendary and Epic books. Each difficulty starts selected with two chests and zero rewards; adjust the chest count to one if needed, and uncheck any difficulty whose rewards were not recorded. Zero explicitly means no rewards of that type. Epic books can be left blank when unknown; Epic counts for older saved records start unknown, not zero. Save one entry per date; selecting an existing date loads it for editing. Recorded days can also be edited or deleted from the history. Entries are saved locally and included in backups.
- The 7- and 30-day views include today and the preceding 6 or 29 calendar days. All-time also excludes future dates. Totals include all recorded difficulties for the selected period, or only the selected difficulty. The daily average divides by recorded days, not calendar days; the 7-day and 30-day figures multiply that average by 7 and 30. Unrecorded dates and unrecorded selected difficulties are excluded, but recorded zero-reward days count. A day is flagged incomplete if a difficulty is missing or has fewer than two chests (for a single-difficulty view, if that difficulty has one chest). Incomplete days remain included in averages and may lower them, so use the difficulty filter to compare like-for-like records. Estimates are observations, not guaranteed rewards or drop rates.
- Epic book totals include known counts even on days where other recorded difficulties have unknown Epic counts. Epic book averages use only days where every recorded difficulty in the selected view has a known Epic count; the coverage figure shows that denominator. When no Epic counts are known the total is shown as a dash. Fill in old entries if you know their actual Epic rewards.
- **Champion Builds:** Add a champion and edit their name, blessing level and build statuses directly in the table; changes save when a field changes. Notes open in a separate editor. Blessings are levels 0–6, defaulting to 0; Books, Masteries, Gear, Glyphs, and Relic default to Needed and can be set to In Progress or Complete. Each of the six items contributes equally to completion; blessing counts as complete only at level 6 and the other items only at Complete. Unknown blessing levels count as incomplete. Blessing colors progress from green at level 0 to dark red at level 6; statuses and completion have their own color cues alongside their text. Older champion builds migrate with blessing set to Unknown for review, and old Unset or Not applicable statuses become Needed. Rank 1 is the highest completion, ties share a rank, and rankings use the full list even when filtered. Click a table heading to sort and use filters underneath to narrow the list; filters can be combined. Rank and completion use exact numbers (completion uses the displayed rounded percentage), names match text, and Notes filters by whether notes exist. Use Clear filters to reset. On screens 1,200 pixels wide or less, expand a champion card to edit its fields, and use Sort and filter for the same controls. Names do not need to be unique. Builds are saved locally and included in Mercy Ledger exports and imports.
- If the app is open in multiple tabs or windows, reload a stale page before saving when warned; this prevents an older copy from overwriting newer data. Export and import backups from the Mercy Ledger page.
- Soulstone projections are not mercy calculations: an official Soulstone mercy system has not been verified. Inventory depends on your own records.

Rules used: [Plarium's mercy system](https://raid-support.plarium.com/hc/en-us/articles/4415727112722-Mercy-system), [community shard thresholds and standard rates](https://ayumilove.net/raid-shadow-legends-mercy-system-explained/), and [Plarium's Altar of Souls guide](https://raid-support.plarium.com/hc/en-us/articles/5874868312092-Guide-Altar-of-Souls). This app is not affiliated with Plarium.
